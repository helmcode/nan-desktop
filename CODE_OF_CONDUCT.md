# Code of conduct

This project is built by and for the NaN community. Keep it that way.

**Our standards:** be kind and constructive; assume good faith; critique code,
not people; keep discussions in the project's channels; help newcomers get
their first PR in.

**Unacceptable:** harassment, hate speech, personal attacks, trolling,
publishing others' private information, sexualized language, or any conduct
that would make someone unwelcome.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits,
code, issues and contributions that violate this policy, and may temporarily
or permanently ban contributors for serious or repeated violations. Reports go
to the maintainers privately (GitHub organization contact) and are handled
confidentially.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant; it applies
inside this repository, its discussions, and its issue tracker.
