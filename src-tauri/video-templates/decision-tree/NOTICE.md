## Modifications

- Converted from a GSAP timeline to CSS `@keyframes` animations only (no JavaScript).
- Restyled and made responsive: nodes and connectors are laid out in percentages so the
  tree adapts to 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 canvases.
- Localized (Spanish and English) and turned every node label, the cursor name and the
  colors into editable variables.
- Replaced the typo-correction and emoji beats with a highlighted chosen path and a
  check badge.
