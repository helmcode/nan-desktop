## Modifications

- Renamed to "Editorial chart". No third-party brand, name, logo or trademark is included.
- Converted from a GSAP timeline and script-built SVG to static markup animated with CSS
  `@keyframes` only (no JavaScript): bars grow, the line is revealed, labels fade in.
- Restyled and made responsive for 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 canvases.
- Localized (Spanish and English); the headline, subtitle, key, source, column labels and
  bar value labels are editable variables, and the colors too.
- The line value labels no longer count up from zero (that needed a script).
